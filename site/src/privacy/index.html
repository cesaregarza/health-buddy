Know where your context goes.

Keeping your own health stores and choosing to share AI context are separate decisions.

Policy status. This source describes this static site and the planned product boundaries. Final companion wording must be checked against the implemented app before distribution. Operator/contact details and hosting logs or cookies remain unresolved until a host is selected. This is not evidence of App Store approval or companion availability.

This static site

The supplied site source uses local assets, synthetic examples and no analytics, external fonts, third-party embeds, account sign-in or health-data submission form. Its only scripted enhancement copies the readiness prompt when you choose to do so. It does not connect to your health stores or send prompt contents to an AI provider.

A future hosting operator may process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses and user agents. Hosting-specific logs, retention, cookies, operator identity and contact must be verified and documented before public policy publication. No public hostname has been selected.

Your own installation

Health Buddy is designed to keep authoritative health stores in your own installation. This includes canonical health records and the personal source, configuration, tests, notes and state needed to maintain your workspace. Access requires application authorization in addition to private-network access.

Your host, backups, optional connectors and any other software you authorize can affect confidentiality. Native personal extensions are trusted code, not a sandbox for hostile code. Do not put secrets or private records in public source, site URLs or issue reports.

Optional AI context

Using Codex or Claude Code may send explicitly selected health context to your chosen AI provider under that provider's terms and your account settings. Review the requested dates, fields and records before sharing. Self-hosting does not mean no data ever leaves your devices.

The planned application should bound agent reads and expose provenance. Optional connectors need separately approved destinations, scopes and secret references. These controls do not justify blanket privacy, compliance or medical claims.

Planned iPhone companion

The native companion is intended to read approved HealthKit types and send them to the exact receiver you pair over authenticated private HTTPS. It does not write to HealthKit. Health access and pairing are explicit owner actions; device grants are separately revocable.

An empty HealthKit read can mean no data or denied read access. The application must not represent it as a measured zero or proof of a permission decision. Pairing secrets must stay out of public URLs, analytics and logs. Restoring a server requires a fresh security epoch and re-pairing.

The implemented phone's precise supported types, retained metadata and retention/deletion controls still require final companion review. There is no signed-build, TestFlight or external release promise on this site.

Control and recovery requirements

Owner-controlled grants, source enablement, explicit changes, encrypted backup and deliberate restore are required product behaviors. Their runtime and device qualification is separate from preparing this website. Consult the matching delivered release documentation when those artifacts exist.
